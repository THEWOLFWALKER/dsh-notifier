# Report a problem

Check [troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.en.md) first. If the problem remains, obtain a diagnostic summary from Notify & Control → Diagnostics.

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/THEWOLFWALKER/dsh-notifier/issues) describing what you did, what you expected and what happened. Include the plugin version, diagnostic summary and steps to reproduce. Hide personal information in screenshots.

Do not share bot tokens, passwords, pairing codes, complete settings files or private conversations.
